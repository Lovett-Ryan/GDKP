# Application Scenario Catalog

Use this catalog to help recognize a scenario, not to restrict one. GDKP serves all knowledge domains and broad product types.

## Knowledge Scenarios

- textbook, monograph, handbook, or paper-led study;
- university course, professional curriculum, exam, or certification preparation;
- research field, literature area, theory, method, or experiment;
- engineering standard, technical specification, legal rule, policy, or regulated practice;
- official documentation, software repository, protocol, platform, or tool ecosystem;
- online course, lecture series, video channel, podcast, or recorded seminar;
- mathematics, natural science, engineering, medicine, social science, business, law, humanities, arts, languages, or everyday-life skill;
- archive, primary-source collection, interview set, dataset, or mixed private records;
- custom or emerging subject that does not fit an existing category.

## Product Scenarios

- software, model, automation, integration, prototype, or reproducible experiment;
- research plan, analysis, report, dataset, benchmark, or publication artifact;
- engineering design, compliance package, operating procedure, or test plan;
- business strategy, market analysis, service design, process, or decision support;
- course, tutorial, guide, knowledge product, or communication asset;
- personal system, life plan, creative work, event, or other broad deliverable;
- any custom result with observable acceptance conditions.

## Hybrid Scenarios

A project may begin as learning and later produce a product, or a product may require targeted learning. Keep reusable knowledge lineage, but define each executable task as knowledge or product so the completion evidence is unambiguous.

## Novel Scenarios

When the user introduces a new scenario, derive its authority sources, proximity sources, version and jurisdiction risks, required capability, output type, and acceptance evidence from the stated goal. Do not force it into AI, research, or engineering examples.
