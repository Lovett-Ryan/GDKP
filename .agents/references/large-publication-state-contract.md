# Large-Publication Forward-State Contract

Use this contract only when `large_publication` is active. It makes the intended order of work executable without introducing a Notion or Obsidian read-back audit and without pretending that word counts can prove educational quality.

## Why This State Exists

Large-publication artifacts are evidence of completed work only when they were created in the right order and remain bound to the same content. A map synthesized from finished headings, a checkpoint created after publication, or a review receipt generated by the authoring script must not satisfy the workflow.

The canonical internal artifact is `LargePublicationRunState`, owned by `knowledge-product-orchestrator`. It is persisted under `.knowledge-product/artifacts/` and updated at each forward transition. The deterministic validator checks ordering, bindings, hashes, role separation, and completion coverage. It does not judge prose quality.

No end-of-run validation can prove an honest history if every record is fabricated afterward. Therefore the orchestrator must run the relevant gate before dispatching the next owner and append the successful validation result to `.knowledge-product/events.jsonl`. A later all-green snapshot cannot replace those prospective gates.

## Forward Lifecycle

```text
map frozen
  -> Packet dispatch allowed
  -> Packet draft checkpointed
  -> Packet accepted
  -> coherent unit assembled
  -> independent semantic review recorded
  -> findings resolved once
  -> exact claims audited against locator-specific evidence
  -> citation projection validated against the audited fact draft
  -> projected reader-facing draft published
  -> full-book completion declared
```

Backward changes are invalidations, not silent rewrites. A changed map makes affected queued or completed Packets stale. A changed assembled draft invalidates its review, claim audit, CitationProjection, and publication state. A changed final fact-bearing draft invalidates its claim audit, CitationProjection, and publication state. A presentation-only citation change invalidates CitationProjection and publication while preserving a claim audit bound to the unchanged fact draft. Recover from the last matching checkpoint.

## Minimal State Shape

`LargePublicationRunState` is a JSON object, optionally wrapped by a `large_publication_run_state` key. It contains:

- `schema_version`: currently `1`;
- `project_id`, `run_id`, `mode`, and current `status`;
- `map`: immutable map identity, revision, SHA-256, freeze time, input artifact types, and whether it was reconstructed from an existing reader-facing snapshot;
- `obligation_ids`: the complete included knowledge-obligation set frozen before authoring;
- `packets`: prospective bounded generation units;
- `units`: coherent reader-facing drafts assembled from accepted Packets;
- `gate_receipts`: the ordered hash-chained receipts returned by completed prospective gates;
- `completion`: pending or complete declaration.

The mode is `new_publication` or `existing_publication_revision`. A new-publication map may depend on requirements, framework, coverage, and evidence-planning artifacts, but never on a generated draft, publication, checkpoint, or claim receipt. A revision map may use a supplied full-book snapshot, but it must still freeze before replacement prose begins.

Each Packet records:

- unique `packet_id` and positive `ordinal`;
- `status`: `queued`, `dispatched`, `checkpointed`, `accepted`, or `stale`;
- non-empty local `obligation_ids` and explicit `depends_on` Packet IDs;
- the frozen map revision and SHA-256;
- `scope_basis`: `obligation_cluster`, `argument_stage`, `context_split`, or `continuation`;
- a `capacity` object containing integer `context_budget_tokens`, `input_tokens_estimate`, `output_tokens_reservation`, and `safety_margin_tokens` whose sum fits the context budget;
- one orchestrator dispatch record;
- at most one author execution for the Packet, with a stable content reference and SHA-256;
- a durable checkpoint bound to that exact draft hash;
- an acceptance time.

A chapter, page, or heading boundary is never a sufficient Packet scope basis. One chapter may fit one Packet only when its complete local obligations genuinely fit the declared capacity. Context pressure creates additional Packets; it does not lower the output reservation or remove obligations.

Each coherent unit records:

- unique `unit_id`, `status`, and its ordered `packet_ids`;
- the assembled canonical draft reference, SHA-256, and assembly time;
- one `DraftQualityReview` produced by `large-publication-architect` in a distinct execution from every author execution that produced the unit;
- review basis `semantic_obligations_and_relationships`, the exact obligation set inspected, relevant relation IDs, and concrete findings;
- at most one bounded repair record plus one disposition for every finding;
- the final canonical draft SHA-256;
- a passing Zotero claim-audit binding to that final hash;
- a passing CitationProjection whose source hash is that final fact-draft hash and whose projected hash identifies the reader-facing draft;
- a successful Notion publication operation binding to the projected draft hash.

Unit statuses are `planned`, `assembled`, `reviewed`, `resolved`, `audited`, `published`, or `stale`. `reviewed` may contain open findings. `resolved` means every finding has one terminal disposition. A unit with no findings may proceed from `reviewed` directly to claim audit. `resolved` does not mean the same draft was reviewed twice.

## Role and Execution Separation

Logical ownership is enforced in state:

- `knowledge-product-orchestrator` creates dispatch records and owns the run state;
- `notion-node-author` creates Packet drafts, bounded repairs, and publication results;
- `large-publication-architect` creates the single semantic review;
- `zotero-source-gate` creates the factual claim-audit receipt.
- `notion-node-author` creates the CitationProjection and the publication result, but deterministic validation must pass before the publication operation.

Every dispatch, author pass, Architect review, repair, and audit has a non-empty execution ID. The review execution ID must differ from all author and repair execution IDs for that unit. A script that derives findings from title counts, word counts, regular expressions, or a hard-coded empty list is not an Architect execution and must not be recorded as one.

## Prospective Validation Gates

Before each prospective gate, persist the exact current state as a new immutable JSON snapshot under `.knowledge-product/artifacts/large-publication-gates/`. Run [validate_large_publication_state.py](../scripts/validate_large_publication_state.py) on that snapshot with the artifact directory as `--snapshot-root`:

```text
python validate_large_publication_state.py SNAPSHOT --snapshot-root ARTIFACTS --gate map-ready
python validate_large_publication_state.py SNAPSHOT --snapshot-root ARTIFACTS --gate packet-dispatch --packet-id PACKET
python validate_large_publication_state.py SNAPSHOT --snapshot-root ARTIFACTS --gate packet-checkpoint --packet-id PACKET
python validate_large_publication_state.py SNAPSHOT --snapshot-root ARTIFACTS --gate review-ready --unit-id UNIT
python validate_large_publication_state.py SNAPSHOT --snapshot-root ARTIFACTS --gate review-recorded --unit-id UNIT
python validate_large_publication_state.py SNAPSHOT --snapshot-root ARTIFACTS --gate claim-audit-ready --unit-id UNIT
python validate_large_publication_state.py SNAPSHOT --snapshot-root ARTIFACTS --gate publish-ready --unit-id UNIT
python validate_large_publication_state.py FINAL_STATE --snapshot-root ARTIFACTS --gate complete
```

The `map-ready` gate is run before the first author dispatch. `packet-dispatch` is run while the selected Packet is still queued. `packet-checkpoint` is run at exact `checkpointed` state, before acceptance and before any dependent Packet is dispatched. `review-ready` is run before Architect invocation, and `review-recorded` at exact `reviewed` state immediately after it. The claim-audit and publication gates run before those external mutations. Gate output includes a canonical receipt bound to the immutable snapshot reference and SHA-256, selected Packet or unit ID, validation time, and previous receipt SHA-256. Append that receipt to both `LargePublicationRunState.gate_receipts` and Kernel events before the next transition. Completion reloads every historical snapshot, verifies its hash and prior receipt prefix, and reruns its exact gate. A later lifecycle state cannot replay an earlier prospective gate, a broken receipt chain is invalid, and the final gate never substitutes for the earlier gates. The terminal `complete` check does not emit a reusable prospective receipt.

For formal factual content, also run [validate_claim_audit.py](../scripts/validate_claim_audit.py) with the exact EvidencePack:

```text
python validate_claim_audit.py DRAFT_CLAIMS --receipt RECEIPT --evidence-pack EVIDENCE_PACK
```

That validator checks that each referenced EvidenceUnit resolves to locator-specific source evidence and that every passing result includes a support assessment. A chapter-level placeholder or generic source-pack reference cannot stand in for exact evidence.

After that audit passes and before `publish-ready`, render and validate the reader-facing citation projection:

```text
python validate_citation_projection.py PROJECTED_DRAFT --source-draft FACT_DRAFT --manifest CITATION_PROJECTION --draft-claims DRAFT_CLAIMS --evidence-pack EVIDENCE_PACK
```

This validator checks publication compilation invariants rather than prose quality: internal source identities are absent, source aliases are contiguous, in-text numbers and References are closed over the same manifest, declared links are clickable, and source-to-claim bindings match the audited evidence. It does not reload Notion or add a second model review.

## Completion Barrier

The `complete` gate requires all of the following:

- the map remained frozen before every author dispatch;
- every required prospective gate has one correctly ordered hash-chained receipt whose time falls between the states it separates;
- every included obligation is scheduled in at least one accepted Packet;
- no Packet is queued, dispatched, checkpointed-only, or stale;
- every accepted Packet belongs to a coherent unit;
- every unit has one independent semantic review of the assembled draft;
- every review finding has one terminal disposition and at most one Author repair;
- every final draft has a passing Zotero claim audit bound to its hash;
- every audited unit has a passing CitationProjection bound to the audited fact draft;
- every required unit has a successful publication operation bound to the projected draft hash;
- the completion declaration is later than all publication results.

The validator deliberately does not use a universal word quota, heading count, paragraph count, or keyword score. Those metrics can reveal suspicious output during diagnosis, but they cannot certify explanation depth or continuity. Semantic review remains a bounded model judgment; the state machine makes its inputs, independence, chronology, and consequences inspectable.
