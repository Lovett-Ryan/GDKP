# Outcome Profiles

Outcome work turns the confirmed goal into evidence of learning or a verified product. Task specifications and dependency graphs are internal execution controls, not user-review documents.

## Knowledge Outcomes

Knowledge learning should be goal-directed and applied. Suitable outcomes include:

- reproduce and explain a valuable repository, paper result, experiment, or technique;
- implement a small analogue that isolates the learned mechanism;
- solve a designed problem set and explain error patterns;
- compare competing theories, methods, policies, or interpretations using evidence;
- teach the concept through a concise explanation or demonstration;
- apply the knowledge to a new case and defend the transfer.

Choose an outcome appropriate to the subject. A mathematics course may use problems and derivations; an engineering topic may use a build or test; history or social science may use source comparison and argument; medicine may require constrained case reasoning without implying clinical authority.

Completion evidence should demonstrate capability, such as passing tests, reproducible results, worked reasoning, an evaluation rubric, or a defensible application. Merely reading sources or producing Notion pages is process evidence, not mastery.

## Product Outcomes

A product project is driven by the requested result. Codex may handle most implementation detail unless the user reserves a decision or learning objective. Define:

- the deliverable and intended user;
- functional and quality acceptance tests;
- constraints, compatibility, risk, and non-goals;
- the exact local or external output location;
- verification evidence.

Examples include software, analyses, plans, specifications, designs, automations, reports, workflows, datasets, or broad real-world artifacts. Keep the user focused on consequential trade-offs and the finished result rather than internal task mechanics.

## Task Model

Outcome Orchestrator internally produces atomic TaskSpecs and an acyclic TaskGraph. Each task is either `knowledge` or `product`, has one executor, explicit inputs and outputs, a verification method, and acceptance evidence. It may reference admitted Zotero evidence when external facts, standards, recommendations, compatibility, or safety constraints matter.

Purely local transformations do not need a fabricated evidence-exemption artifact. Record evidence applicability internally and proceed.

## Execution Authority

Reversible work inside the confirmed project and output scope may proceed autonomously. Ask before destructive operations, user-content overwrite, material cost, credential use beyond the confirmed boundary, external communication, or a goal-changing choice.

Do not publish task queues, execution state, validation logs, or project questions to Notion. Notion receives only a final product publication when the user requested one. The local project and its actual deliverables remain the product truth source.

## Validation

OutcomeEvidence must point to the real artifact, test, result, rubric, or demonstration that satisfies each acceptance condition. Read back external results and run relevant local checks. Report incomplete or failed acceptance conditions plainly; do not convert activity into completion.
